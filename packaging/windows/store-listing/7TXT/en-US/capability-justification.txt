7TXT is a Tauri/Rust desktop editor. runFullTrust enables WebView2, native dialogs, user-selected files, clipboard, recovery, and PDF/image viewing. Optional Store checks and the Store-page link run at current-user privileges. No elevation, service, startup task, background agent, or virtualization exclusions. Standard MSIX virtualization, Windows 11 x64. Daily checks are off by default; no silent installation or document transmission.
